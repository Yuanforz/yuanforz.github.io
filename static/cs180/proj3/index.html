B

Flow matching from scratch

Learning the field

A small UNet trained from scratch on MNIST: first a one-step denoiser, then time- and class-conditioned flow matching.

MNIST: 60,000 TRAIN / 10,000 TEST28 × 28

B1.1

UNet

Down to a 1 × 1 bottleneck and back up, with skip connections at each resolution. D = 128 for the denoisers, 64 for the flow models.

28 × 2801 / ConvBlock · D
14 × 1402 / DownBlock · D
7 × 703 / DownBlock · 2D
1 × 104 / AvgPool · 2D
7 × 705 / Unflatten · 2D
14 × 1406 / UpBlock · D
28 × 2807 / UpBlock · D
28 × 2808 / Output · 1 channel

B2.1 B2.2 B2.3

Time-conditioned flow matching

Predict the velocity x1 − x0 from xt and t; t enters through two FCBlocks.

xt = (1 − t)x0 + tx1
L = E ‖uθ(xt,t) − (x1 − x0)‖²

D = 64, batch 64, Adam at lr 0.01 decaying to 0.001 over 10 epochs. Sampling: 50 Euler steps.
